# Evidence rules

- A: official documentation, original API response, original paper, or controlled vocabulary.
- B: research-institution guidance or a method paper read through a reliable source.
- C: community or media lead that still needs verification.
- U: model-generated candidate without external evidence.

Keep term evidence separate from retrieval testing. A term observed in a title or abstract is not automatically equivalent to a canonical concept and does not prove retrieval gain.
